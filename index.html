Trained as a media designer. Alongside that, I founded house music event brands in 2022 and have led their visual side since 2025. Everything the curriculum didn't cover, I taught myself.

Started freelancing during my apprenticeship in 2025. In 2026, I built a few small web projects on the side: this portfolio and my workflow dashboard.

I'm looking for an in-house place where I can look after a brand properly: print, social, campaigns, whatever the team needs. I bring the energy, curiosity and range that got me here.

Got a project, or just curious? Let's talk.
