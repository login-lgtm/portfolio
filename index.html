Trained as a media designer — alongside that, founded house music event brands in 2022 and have been co-running them since 2024. Everything the curriculum didn't cover, I taught myself.

Started freelancing during my apprenticeship in 2025. In 2026, I built a handful of web projects — this portfolio and my workflow dashboard.

I'm not chasing one narrow title. I'm looking for a place where I can keep building and crafting — digital, print, whatever the team needs — and bring the energy, curiosity, and range that got me here.

Got a project — or just curious? Let's talk.
